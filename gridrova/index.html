GridRova

That lovely moment when a hidden word comes into focus: one last letter, a quick trace, and there it is.

GridRova turns that small discovery into a scenic word-search journey through 300 levels.

Coming soon to Google Play

What's inside

  • 300 levels
  • 30 scenic stops
  • Postcards to collect
  • Daily puzzles from level 10
  • Time challenges from level 20
  • Plays offline
  • No purchases

Start with simple puzzles, then explore larger grids, longer words and more directions. Themes from animals and food to space and ocean life keep each chapter fresh. Stuck? Use a recharging nudge, or spend in-game coins on hints. Coins are earned by playing and can't be bought.

GridRova is free and shows ads. Players under 13, and anyone who prefers not to give their age, only see child-safe ads. Optional online rankings use a random name, never an email address.
