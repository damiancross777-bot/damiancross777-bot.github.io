Delete your GridRova data

GridRova by Phoneporium.AU. Last updated 22 October 2026.

You don't enter a username, password or email to play GridRova. If you played online rankings, the game created an anonymous player account for you, and our server holds its ID, a random player name, your ranked results and any prize records. You can delete the account and that data.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open GridRova and go to Settings.
  2. Choose "Delete my online data" and confirm.
  3. Your anonymous account, player name, ranked attempts and results, and coin prize records are removed from our server straight away, and the account can no longer sign in.

Ask us by email

If you no longer have the game, email phoneporium.au@gmail.com with the subject "GridRova data deletion" and your player name exactly as it appeared in the game. We'll reply within 7 days.

There's no password or email linked to an anonymous account, so we can't check who owns it. To protect current players, we delete by email only when exactly one account has that player name and it hasn't been used for at least 30 days. If it's been used more recently, please delete it from Settings on the phone that has the game. We never ask for passwords or payment details.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your anonymous account and its sign-in, player name, ranked attempts and results, and coin prize records.
  • Kept: published final daily results keep your place so other players' ranks stay correct, with your name replaced by "Deleted player". A record of the deleted account ID and deletion time is kept for 30 days so a prize can't be paid to a deleted account, then removed automatically.
  • Server request logs held by Google Cloud are deleted automatically after 30 days. Google Cloud's administrative audit records, such as the fact that an account was deleted, are kept by Google for 400 days.
  • Records Google AdMob handles under its own policy aren't covered by this deletion. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Progress, coins and medals saved on your phone aren't affected. To remove them, uninstall GridRova or clear its storage in Android Settings.
