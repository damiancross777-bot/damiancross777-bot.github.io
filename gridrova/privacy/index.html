GridRova privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026. Applies to GridRova version 1.0 for Android.

The short version

  • You don't enter a username, password or email to play, and we never ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress, coins and medals are saved on your phone.
  • When the game first opens it asks what year you were born. Only an age group is kept, on your phone, to choose suitable ad settings.
  • Players under 13, and anyone who chooses "Prefer not to say", get Google's most protective ad settings: non-personalised ads rated G.
  • If you choose to play online rankings, an anonymous player account is created for you. You can delete it, and its online data, from the game at any time.
  • We don't sell your information. GridRova has no in-app purchases and no separate analytics tool of our own.

Who we are

GridRova is made by Phoneporium.AU, an independent game developer in Australia. We are responsible for the information described in this policy. You can contact us at phoneporium.au@gmail.com.

Who GridRova is for

GridRova is intended for players aged 13 and older. It isn't designed for or directed at children. The game still asks every player's birth year, and younger or unknown-age players get the more protective ad settings described below. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child's online data at any time.

Information kept on your phone

GridRova saves the following on your device so you can carry on where you left off: level progress, any puzzle in progress, coins, medals, postcards, settings (sound, music, grid lines) and your age group. This information isn't sent to us. It is removed if you uninstall the game or clear its storage. A backup file you choose to export is yours to keep or delete.

The age question

The first time you open GridRova, it asks "What year were you born?" with nothing preselected, and a "Prefer not to say" option. The birth year is used only on your phone, at that moment, to work out an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over). Only the age group and when it was chosen are saved, on your phone. The birth year itself is not stored or sent anywhere.

The age group is used only to set up ads (see below). You can switch to child-safe ads at any time in Settings. The game won't switch from child-safe ads back to normal ads.

Advertising

GridRova is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are not requested until the age question has been answered.

Age groupWhat the game asks Google for
Under 13, or "Prefer not to say"Ad requests are marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, and ask for non-personalised ads rated G.
13 to 17Ad requests are marked as under the age of consent and ask for non-personalised ads rated PG.
18 and overGoogle's consent message is shown where the law requires it. With your consent, ads may be personalised; otherwise they are non-personalised. Ads are rated PG or lower.

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob processes information such as your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, app performance diagnostics and device identifiers such as the advertising ID. For child-directed and under-age requests, Google limits how this information is used. Google handles this information under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Online rankings

Online rankings are optional. If you choose to play them, Google Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous player account for you. There is no username, password or email, and the account's sign-in key is stored securely on your phone. We use the account's identifier to link your ranked results and any in-game prize records to you.

For each ranked puzzle you play, our server keeps:

  • your anonymous player ID and a randomly generated player name, which you didn't choose and which doesn't identify you
  • when you started and finished, the word paths you submitted, your time, your result and your rank
  • receipts for any in-game coin prizes awarded for a daily podium place (coins have no cash value)

Your random player name, rank and time are shown publicly on the daily rankings. Our server runs on Google Cloud in Australia (Sydney region). Google keeps request logs, which include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

Version 1.0 has no account linking, email sign-in or cloud save. Because the anonymous account's key lives only on your phone, it can't be moved to or recovered on another phone, and your game progress is never uploaded.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • We don't include a separate analytics tool of our own. Google AdMob processes ad interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads, as described above.
  • The game doesn't request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or file storage permissions.
  • There are no in-app purchases or chat features.

How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Online rankings data: until you delete it.
  • Published final daily results: kept so past podiums stay correct. If you delete your data, your place stays but your name is replaced with "Deleted player".
  • Deletion marker: a record of your anonymous ID and when it was deleted, kept for 30 days so a prize can't be paid to a deleted account, then removed automatically.
  • Server request logs (which include IP addresses): deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Google Cloud's administrative audit records for our server (for example, that an account was deleted): kept by Google for 400 days. This period is set by Google and can't be shortened.

Deleting your data

In GridRova, open Settings and choose "Delete my online data". This removes your anonymous player account and its ranking, attempt and prize records straight away. Published final standings keep your place with the name "Deleted player", and a short-lived deletion marker is kept as described above. Game progress, coins and medals on your phone aren't affected. Deleting online data doesn't erase records Google AdMob handles under its own policy, or backup files you exported. If you no longer have the game, see delete your data.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing phoneporium.au@gmail.com. We'll reply within 7 days. Because we don't collect names or email addresses through the game, we'll ask for the player name shown in the game so we can find your data.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. If you live elsewhere, you may also have rights under your local law, such as the GDPR in Europe and the UK.

Information sent overseas

Ad requests are handled by Google, which may process information in other countries, including the United States. Our rankings server is hosted in Australia.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, such as adding email sign-in or cloud save, we'll also say so in the game before it takes effect.
